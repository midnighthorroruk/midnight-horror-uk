Privacy Policy — Midnight Horror UK

Effective date: 25 October 2026

Midnight Horror UK is a TikTok developer application used by its creator to publish the creator's own original horror story videos to the creator's own TikTok account (@midnighthorroruk.tv) through TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API.

Data collected

The app uses TikTok's official authorization flow. It accesses only the data needed to connect the account and publish videos: basic account information and the videos the owner chooses to upload. The app does not collect passwords.

How data is used

Data is used solely to authenticate the owner and to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account. Data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for advertising.

Data retention

Access tokens are stored only for as long as needed to operate the app and can be revoked by the owner at any time through TikTok's settings.

Contact

Contact the developer through the TikTok account @midnighthorroruk.tv.
